# TGFile

## English

**TGFile** is a clean, efficient, and versatile iOS app that brings Telegram file management and media playback together.

Some of its features include:

1. **Save and forward restricted content**  
   Save and forward content from restricted channels and groups that your account can access.

2. **Customize file thumbnails**  
   Choose custom thumbnails when sending or forwarding files.

3. **Turn files into media messages**  
   Forward media sent as files as photos, videos, or audio, with spoiler options for photos and videos. Preview or play media files directly without waiting for the full download.

4. **Optimized file transfers**  
   Improved download and upload performance for faster, smoother transfers.

5. **Multiple accounts and bots**  
   Manage multiple Telegram accounts and bots in one app.

6. **Versatile media playback**  
   Watch videos, listen to audio, and view images directly in the app, with support for a wide range of formats.

7. **Background transfers**  
   Support for downloading, uploading, and forwarding in the background.

8. **Multilingual interface**  
   Use TGFile in your preferred language.

Requires **iOS 17 or later**.

**Support and security inquiries:** [sidekite@zhosix.com](mailto:sidekite@zhosix.com)

## 简体中文

**TGFile**，一款简洁、高效、全面的 iOS 工具，将 Telegram 文件管理与媒体播放融为一体。

部分功能如下：

1. **保存与转发受限内容**  
   支持保存、转发当前账户可访问的受限频道与群组内容。

2. **自定义文件缩略图**  
   发送或转发文件时，可自行设置缩略图，让文件展示更直观。

3. **文件与媒体形式灵活转换**  
   以文件形式发送的媒体，可重新作为图片、视频或音频转发，图片和视频支持防剧透。文件形式的媒体也能直接在线播放或查看，无需等待完整下载。

4. **优化文件传输效率**  
   优化下载与上传性能，让文件传输更快速、更顺畅。

5. **支持多账户与机器人**  
   在同一个 App 中管理多个 Telegram 账户与机器人。

6. **丰富的媒体播放与查看功能**  
   直接在 App 内播放视频、音频与查看图片，支持多种媒体格式。

7. **支持后台传输**  
   支持后台下载、上传与转发，切换应用也能继续处理任务。

8. **支持多种语言**  
   提供多语言界面，按自己的使用习惯选择。

支持 **iOS 17 及以上版本**。

**支持与安全问题：** [sidekite@zhosix.com](mailto:sidekite@zhosix.com)
